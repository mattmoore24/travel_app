# Community Guidelines (DRAFT)

> **Status: draft for founder review. Not yet published. Legal review is a
> separate, required step before launch.**

This app exists so travelers can make friends. Keep it casual and friendly
and you will never think about these rules again.

## The one big rule

**Keep it casual and friendly.** Explicit or sexual content is not allowed.
If you wouldn't say it to someone you had just met in a hostel common room,
don't send it.

## Moderation

- **Every first message is screened before it's delivered.** Messages that
  are explicit, harassing, or spam are blocked — the recipient never sees
  them.
- **Repeated problems have consequences.** Blocked messages and removed
  photos are recorded, and repeat offenders lose access to the app.
- **Reports are reviewed.** Every report is logged with evidence and
  reviewed. Blocking someone instantly hides you from each other everywhere.

## Also not allowed

- Harassment, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind
- Impersonation, fake profiles, or photos that aren't you
- Spam, commercial solicitation, or scams (including "come to my bar" shills)
- Sharing someone else's private information
- Explicit, suggestive, or violent photos
- Anyone under 18

## What we ask of you

- Use recent photos that are actually you (verify with a selfie for the badge)
- Make plans in public places; tell someone where you're going
- Report anything that feels off — you're never wasting our time

## Contact

Questions, appeals, or safety concerns: use **Contact us** in the app
(Guidelines screen), which reaches a real person and lets us reply to you.

_(A dedicated support address still has to exist for the App Store listing
and the privacy policy: `SUPPORT_INBOX` in the deploy secrets.)_
